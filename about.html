About Niksprojects
A student-first platform built to make learning and building easier.

Built by a Student, for Students

Niksprojects started as a simple idea — what if students could access real, working projects to learn from, submit, or build on top of? No more starting from scratch every time.

We curate and build high-quality projects across web, mobile, and full-stack domains, complete with source code, documentation, and setup guides so you can hit the ground running.

Whether you need a ready-made project or want something custom-built to your exact specs, we've got you covered.
