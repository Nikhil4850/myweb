Privacy Policy
Last updated: May 2026

At Niksprojects Store, your privacy matters. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your data.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly when you:

  • Make a purchase (name, email, payment details via Razorpay)
  • Submit a contact or custom order form (name, email, phone, message)
  • Sign up for updates or newsletters

We may also collect basic usage data automatically, such as browser type, pages visited, and referring URLs, to improve the site experience.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To process and fulfill your orders
  • To respond to support requests and inquiries
  • To send order confirmations and download links
  • To improve our website and services
  • To send occasional updates (only if you opt in)

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.

3. Payment Information

All payments are processed securely through Razorpay. We do not store your card or UPI details on our servers. Razorpay's own privacy policy governs how your payment data is handled.

4. Cookies

We use minimal cookies and browser localStorage to remember your preferences and improve your browsing experience. No tracking cookies or third-party ad cookies are used.

5. Data Sharing

We only share your data with:

  • Razorpay — for payment processing
  • Service providers who help us operate the site (e.g., hosting)

All third parties are required to keep your information confidential and use it only for the services they provide to us.

6. Data Retention

We retain your information only as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this policy, or as required by law. You can request deletion of your data at any time.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Opt out of marketing communications at any time

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at niksprojectss@gmail.com.

8. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information, including SSL encryption on all pages. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

9. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. Continued use of the site after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

11. Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach us at:

  • Email: niksprojectss@gmail.com
  • Via the Contact page
